VANTABLACK: CARBON DATA

The Text Adventure Void That Stares Back

You wake in a place without light—
the kind that doesn't end when you close your eyes.
This world is stitched from memory, noise, and half-deleted dreams.
Somewhere in the static, someone—or something—is waiting to be found.
Let the signal-to-noise ratio be your only compass.

How to Play

NAVIGATION

  • MOVE works even in total darkness - feel your way through the void
  • Try: move forward, walk around, feel your way

DISCOVERY

  • EXAMINE only works once there's light
  • When the dark remembers your face, look closer at what it was hiding
  • Some objects reveal themselves only after you've completed a puzzle (ie, light the room)

SENSORY EXPLORATION

  • LISTEN - hear what the darkness is trying to tell you
  • TOUCH - feel the cold porcelain, the smooth surfaces
  • SMELL - the stale air holds secrets
  • TASTE - Use at your own risk
  • Use your body like a sensor when sight fails

COMMUNICATION

  • SAY - speak into the void, but be careful what you say
  • Someone - or something - might be listening
  • The right words can unlock hidden responses

THE DEVICE

  • Meet your 90's AI Assistant, Patch. Hints available.
